1. Current School Canteen Challenges vs. Smart Food App

❌ Current Canteen Bottlenecks

  • Chaotic Recess Queues: Students lose 10–12 mins of their 15-min break in crowds.
  • Cash Loss & Hygiene Risks: Cash mishandling and counter disputes among young kids.
  • Zero Allergy Visibility: Canteen staff unaware of medical nut, dairy, or gluten sensitivities.
  • 30–40% Food Wastage: Kitchens cook on guesswork, causing high waste or stockouts.

✅ Smart Food App Transformation

  • Classroom Desk Delivery: Pre-ordered hot meals delivered to desks before the bell.
  • 100% Cashless Campus: 1-Tap Campus Lunch Wallet & instant UPI payments.
  • Active Allergy Shield: Hard stops on allergen conflicts + bold warnings on stickers.
  • Pre-Order Demand Forecasting: Kitchens cook exact pre-ordered batches by cutoff.
